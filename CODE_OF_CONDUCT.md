# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in VERITY a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of
experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Accepting feedback and explaining technical disagreements.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Protecting user privacy and avoiding credential disclosure.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, personal
attacks, sexualized language, sustained disruption, and publishing others'
private information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards.
They may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of
Conduct. Report concerns privately through GitHub's repository moderation tools
or the security contact described in `SECURITY.md`.
