## Becoming a maintainer

Additional maintainers must be named by the repository owner and added to
`GOVERNANCE.md`, `MAINTAINERS.md`, `.github/CODEOWNERS`, and security response
documentation. No maintainer role is inferred from issue or pull-request
activity alone.
